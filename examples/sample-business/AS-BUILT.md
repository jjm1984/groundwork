# As-Built — Riverside Parts Counter

## What this is

A single-location auto-parts counter app: look up a part by number, log the sale. Nothing else — no accounting, no ordering.

## The story so far

### 2026-09-20 — First setup

Set up with `prompts/setup-new-project.md`. Dave (the owner) answered the 5 business questions directly; manual part-number entry was kept on purpose rather than adding a barcode scanner, since the counter staff type the number in anyway while talking to the customer. See `decisions/dec-001-what-this-does.md` and `dec-002-no-scanner-yet.md`.

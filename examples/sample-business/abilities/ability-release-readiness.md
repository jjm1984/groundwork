# Ability: Release readiness

## Goal

A change that touches stock counts or sale logging doesn't go live with an error that would misstate stock or lose a sale record.

## Serves

`roles/role-reviewer.md`.

## Uses

`skills/devils-advocate` (before trusting a change that "looks right"), `skills/write-a-decision-record` (so what shipped and why is on record, not just in the commit message).

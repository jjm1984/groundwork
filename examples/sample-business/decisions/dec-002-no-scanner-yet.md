# dec-002: No barcode scanner integration for now

## Context

Q5 of `setup-new-project.md` ("anything already known-temporary you don't want fixed") — owner flagged manual part-number entry as a known limitation, not a defect.

## Options considered

1. Build scanner support now.
2. Leave manual entry, revisit later.

## Decision

Option 2. Manual entry stays as-is.

## Why

Owner's words: "the counter guys type the number in anyway while they're talking to the customer, a scanner won't actually speed that up." Not a technical limitation — a considered call about how the counter actually works day to day.
